The cycle (whatever applies today, in this order, 3 s pause between):
  WELCOME  >  HAPPY BIRTHDAY  >  HAPPY ANNIVERSARY
  Each kind: names shuffled, 3 s each (at least 10 s per kind: 1 name = 10 s, 2 = 5 s each),
  gif changes every 10 s (shuffled deck, no repeats until all used, never the same twice in a row),
  then fades out; after the last kind everything is gone until the app is reopened.
  Tapping the row opens the popup for the kind showing (its gif, title, names A-Z, its backdrop);
  the clock pauses while it is open. During the 3 s pause the row is not tappable.
  Button layout (no room for a row): the button shows 10 s per kind with that kind's icon.

The rules (celebrationsFromMembers in js/birthdayParty.js, covered by tests)
  WELCOME      added today or in the 6 days before (7 days in all; WELCOME_DAYS).
               Not future dates, not 7+ days ago.
  BIRTHDAY     month/day of "birthday" is today (year optional: "1990-11-13" or "11-13").
  ANNIVERSARY  month/day of "added" is today and at least 1 year ago; years are NOT shown for ordinary anniversaries (name only); only 5/10/15... year milestones show them.
               Someone added today is a welcome, not an anniversary.
               29 Feb dates are celebrated on 28 Feb in years with no 29th.
  Milestones   every 5th year (5, 10, 15, ...): the title becomes "10-YEAR ANNIVERSARY" and the person
               gets two beats - the name, then special text ("A whole decade!"); in the popup they are
               starred and gold. Wording is MILESTONE_TEXT at the top of the script
               (5 Half a decade! / 10 A whole decade! / 25 A quarter century! / 50 Golden jubilee!,
               other multiples of 5: "<n> years strong!").
  Dates are "YYYY-MM-DD". "Today" is the device date in the demo; in the live data it is Trinidad's date (worked out by the Worker).

LIVE DATA (new in v2.1.0)
  On every app opening js/birthdayParty.js fetches ONE static file (unless a ?birthdaydemo= is in the address):
      https://media.mmicj.com/catalog/celebrations.json?d=<today's Trinidad date>
      {"v":1, "date":"2026-11-14", "welcome":[{"name":"Ann"}], "birthday":["Joy"],
       "anniversary":[{"name":"Bo"}, {"name":"Cy", "years":5}]}
  - It is a static file, so it costs no Worker request. ?d=<date> gives each day its own saved copy.
  - It is used ONLY if its "date" equals today's date in Trinidad (UTC-4, worked out from the clock, so the
    phone's time zone does not matter). Yesterday's file, a 404, offline, bad JSON, an 8 s timeout: silently nothing.
  - Names only. The portal's Worker writes it (nightly + after every Members save). See the portal's
    docs/MEMBERS-CELEBRATIONS.md. Years appear only on every 5th anniversary, so ordinary anniversaries show the name only.
  - Switch it off: LIVE_ON = false at the top of the script. The file address is TODAY_URL.
  - BirthdayParty.loadToday() runs it again from the console (it only shows if nothing has played yet).
  - The service worker only touches page loads, so it never caches this file.
  Go-live checklist: delete assets/birthday/members-demo.json (demo only), add the welcome / anniversary gifs and icons.

The shape the app takes (also what the Worker writes)
  BirthdayParty.setCelebrations({
      welcome:     ["Ann", ...],                       // names
      birthday:    ["Joy", ...],                       // names
      anniversary: [{name:"Daniel", years:5}, {name:"Ruth"}, ...]   // years only needed for 5/10/15... milestones
  });

Cost / risk
  Nothing runs without names. Otherwise: one small manifest.json per kind that plays, plus the gifs.
  Touches no other front-page code. Rollback: remove the <link> and <script> lines from index.html.
