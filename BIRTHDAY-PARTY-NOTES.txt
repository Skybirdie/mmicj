The cycle (whatever applies today, in this order, 3 s pause between):
  WELCOME  >  HAPPY BIRTHDAY  >  HAPPY ANNIVERSARY
  Each kind: names shuffled, 3 s each (at least 10 s per kind: 1 name = 10 s, 2 = 5 s each),
  gif changes every 10 s (shuffled deck, no repeats until all used, never the same twice in a row),
  then fades out; after the last kind everything is gone until the app is reopened.
  Tapping the row opens the popup for the kind showing (its gif, title, names A-Z, its backdrop);
  the clock pauses while it is open. During the 3 s pause the row is not tappable.
  Button layout (no room for a row): the button shows 10 s per kind with that kind's icon.

The rules (celebrationsFromMembers in js/birthdayParty.js, covered by tests)
  WELCOME      added today or in the 6 days before (7 days in all; WELCOME_DAYS).
               Not future dates, not 7+ days ago.
  BIRTHDAY     month/day of "birthday" is today (year optional: "1990-11-13" or "11-13").
  ANNIVERSARY  month/day of "added" is today and at least 1 year ago; "3 years" shows after the name.
               Someone added today is a welcome, not an anniversary.
               29 Feb dates are celebrated on 28 Feb in years with no 29th.
  Milestones   every 5th year (5, 10, 15, ...): the title becomes "10-YEAR ANNIVERSARY" and the person
               gets two beats - the name, then special text ("A whole decade!"); in the popup they are
               starred and gold. Wording is MILESTONE_TEXT at the top of the script
               (5 Half a decade! / 10 A whole decade! / 25 A quarter century! / 50 Golden jubilee!,
               other multiples of 5: "<n> years strong!").
  Dates are "YYYY-MM-DD". "Today" is the device date in the demo; the real data should be worked out
  for Trinidad's date by the daily Worker.
