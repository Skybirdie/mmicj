Installed app stays inside the device's top and bottom bars
============================================================
Changed files (drop into the project, same paths):
  index.html                 (2 lines: links css/installed-frame.css and js/installedFrame.js)
  css/installed-frame.css    NEW
  js/installedFrame.js       NEW

Cause: #app was sized 100vw x 100dvh. In an installed (standalone) window that
can be taller than the visible window; <body> is flex-centred with overflow:hidden,
so the overshoot was cropped equally at the top and bottom, hiding the top bar,
the page indicator / status bar and the welcome bar under the device bars.

Fix: when installed, js/installedFrame.js measures the real visible height
(smaller of layout viewport and window inner height) into --mm-app-h and keeps it
updated on resize / rotation. installed-frame.css sizes #app, #app.viewerFocus,
the phone library drawer, the Video and Slideshow sections and the slideshow stage
from it. Video/Slideshow also start below the device top bar (env(safe-area-inset-top)).

Not affected: regular browser tabs (no class, no variable, no rule matches), Front Page,
Share Mode, storage, network, Worker. Rollback = remove the two lines from index.html.
